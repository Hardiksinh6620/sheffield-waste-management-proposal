# Research questions and evidence traceability

The proposal's aims appear on PDF page 1. This matrix translates the proposed aims into the evidence needed to answer them; it does not assert that the evidence was collected.

| Proposed aim | Evidence needed | Suitable deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Assess existing practice | Defined performance indicators, period, and comparison basis | An assessment with its denominator and scope |
| Map waste-management patterns | Located observations, attribute definitions, and geographic reference | Maps with documented inputs and methods |
| Identify improvements | Findings linked to practical constraints | Recommendations traceable to evidence |
| Consider transfer to other places | Comparison of local conditions and data availability | A bounded discussion of transferability |
| Encourage public awareness | Defined audience and engagement approach | A communication plan, not an assumed measured effect |

Keep perception measures distinct from operational measures. Agreement with a statement is not itself a measured change in collection efficiency. Record what would disconfirm a proposed explanation as well as what would support it.
