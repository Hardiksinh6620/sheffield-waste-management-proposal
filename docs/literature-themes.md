# Literature themes in the proposal

PDF pages 2-6 discuss studies of GIS use, route planning, landfill suitability, urban waste demand, and environmental considerations. The following is a guide to the proposal's discussion; the cited papers have not been newly reviewed in full for this repository.

| Theme | Role in the proposed study | Transfer question |
|---|---|---|
| GIS adoption and practitioner views | Explains perceived benefits and barriers | Are the same constraints present in Sheffield? |
| Collection and transport routing | Motivates efficiency questions | Are network, vehicle, and service constraints available? |
| Landfill suitability and multicriteria analysis | Shows how spatial criteria can guide choices | How were criteria and weights justified? |
| Urban dynamics and waste demand | Connects changing settlements with service needs | Do time periods and spatial units match? |
| Environmental effects | Broadens evaluation beyond operational cost | What impact measures are actually observed? |

Before using any literature result as a numerical benchmark, check the original source, setting, methods, and uncertainty. The bibliography on pages 7-9 remains the source record; some entries require cleaning and verification. Do not import another city's findings as Sheffield measurements.
